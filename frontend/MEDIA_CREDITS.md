## Founder portrait

The active founder portrait is `public/media/made-in-india-2026/kirthika-founder-portrait.png`. The project owner supplied `1782155451259.png` directly and authorized its use for Kirthika's founder profile. The 800 × 800 PNG was copied unchanged, preserving the beige blazer and original beige background. No retouching, background removal, or other image edits were applied.

### Previous portrait assets (inactive)

The earlier founder portrait used the owner-selected `0W0A5432.JPG`. The [source photograph](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mYjD7-Lf6mQvrMMnfZ2AhILDeDgClruf/view) was edited with the built-in `image_gen` tool in background-extraction mode. The output is `public/media/made-in-india-2026/kirthika-profile-cutout.png` (1024 × 1535), with a verified alpha channel. The original Drive file is unchanged. This portrait was presented as a waist-up crop inside a CSS purple arch with layered decorative edges and a subtle hover accent. The cutout and subsequent retouched versions below are retained as inactive backups and are no longer displayed.

Final editing prompt:

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied photograph 0W0A5432.JPG. Create a genuinely transparent PNG cutout of the single woman wearing the orange-red sari and black sleeveless blouse. Remove the entire room, table, banners, lettering, and all background objects. Keep only the woman and items she is wearing or holding, including her purple lanyard, badge, jewelry, and phone. Preserve her exact face, facial features, expression, natural skin texture, hairstyle, body proportions, pose, hands, clothing colors, and fabric texture. Do not beautify, reshape, relight, stylize, or invent missing body parts. Preserve delicate hair edges without a white halo. Crop the canvas to a vertical portrait around her visible silhouette, with a little transparent space above and beside her; retain the original lower crop at the bottom of the photograph. No backdrop, border, shadow, graphics, or new text. Output actual alpha transparency, not a checkerboard image.

### Previous subtle portrait retouch (inactive)

At the project owner's request, a light facial-lighting retouch was made using the built-in `image_gen` tool in identity-preserve mode. This inactive intermediate asset is `public/media/made-in-india-2026/kirthika-profile-retouched.png`. Transparency was retained, along with the previous `kirthika-profile-cutout.png` backup. The CSS waist-up crop and decorative frame were unchanged at that stage.

Retouch prompt:

Make an extremely subtle, natural photographic retouch of the face only: gently soften harsh under-eye and facial shadows, reduce small shiny highlights, and slightly even uneven lighting. This should look like better soft portrait lighting, not a different or beautified face. Preserve EXACT facial geometry, eye shape and size, nose, lips, jawline, cheeks, eyebrows, expression, gaze, natural skin tone, age, pores and fine skin texture. Keep existing makeup unchanged. No face slimming, reshaping, skin lightening, airbrushing, added makeup, enlarged eyes, new smile, or altered teeth. Keep hair, clothing, hands, jewelry, lanyard, badge, phone, body, pose, silhouette, framing, canvas proportions and all other areas unchanged. Preserve the existing transparent alpha background; no new background, border, shadow or text. Use the original pixels as faithfully as possible; the improvement must be barely noticeable and confined to gentle tonal balancing.

### Previous subtle facial contour adjustment (inactive)

The project owner requested a slightly slimmer face with minimal visible change. The built-in `image_gen` tool was used in identity-preserve mode. This inactive asset is `public/media/made-in-india-2026/kirthika-profile-refined.png`. The previous retouch is also retained as a backup. Preserve her recognizable appearance, natural asymmetry, age, skin tone and texture, expression, gaze, eyes, brows, nose, lips, forehead, chin length, makeup, and current lighting.
